First the book, since every figure comes from it: import every broker you use, from your first trade; fix what **Import** flags as needing a fix; pick the market, on its ticker page, for a name counted at cost because no price was found; and upload your latest statement when your newest transaction is old. Then switch on what works for you: **My plan** in Profile (target mix and caps, which the daily briefing and Sell or add hold your book to), **exit rules** per position on Portfolio, your **tax residence** for the tax report, your **investor profile** so the assistant frames answers for your risk and horizon, **notifications** for alerts and the daily summary, and Profile's **MCP** tab to use your book from Claude or ChatGPT. The guided tour shows which of these your account already has on and takes you to each.
