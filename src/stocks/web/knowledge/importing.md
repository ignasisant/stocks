The Import page turns a broker statement into transactions in your ledger. Positions, P/L, dividends, fees and tax reports on the Portfolio page are all derived from that ledger.

## Supported brokers and how the format is detected
The Import page reads statements from Revolut (CSV or PDF), Revolut crypto (CSV), Trading 212 (CSV), DEGIRO (CSV), Interactive Brokers (CSV), ClickTrade / Saxo (Excel or CSV) and a Generic CSV in the app's own layout. Any crypto exchange's export also works (see the crypto section). You pick a platform on the Import page, but the app does not rely on that pick: it tries the built-in parsers on the file, and the assistant also reads it as a cross-check. If the file is really from another supported broker, the page says "Read as X, not Y". If no parser knows the layout (a different broker, a hand-kept spreadsheet, a PDF from another broker), the AI works out the columns and the page says "Read by the AI ... Check the rows before you commit them". A holdings report with no dated trades is refused as "nothing to import": export the transactions statement instead. Accepted file types are CSV, XLSX and PDF, up to 50 MB.

## What to export from Revolut, Trading 212 and the generic CSV
- Revolut (stocks): export the trading statement from Revolut, Stocks, statement. CSV is exact; PDF also works (older and 2026 layouts, English or Spanish). Cash movements, rewards and fee rows are skipped; splits are derived from share counts when possible, else add them by hand. Revolut CSV does not break out dividend withholding tax.
- Revolut crypto: export the crypto statement as CSV (details in the crypto section).
- Trading 212: History, Export (CSV). Only accepted with its exact columns (Action, Time, ISIN, Ticker, No. of shares, Price / share). Trades are recorded in the instrument's currency; fees import as 0. Dividend withholding tax becomes the fee when reported in the same currency. Deposits, interest and currency conversions are skipped.
- Generic CSV: header `date,ticker,action,quantity,price,currency,fee,note`. Actions: buy, sell, dividend, fee, split, capital, transfer_in, transfer_out. Date, ticker and action are required; currency defaults to USD. A dividend's total goes in the price column. Write 1.5, not 1,5.

## What to export from DEGIRO, Interactive Brokers and ClickTrade / Saxo
- DEGIRO: Activity, Transactions, Export, CSV (English or Spanish headers; needs Date, Product, ISIN, Quantity, Price). Portfolio.csv (holdings) and Account.csv (cash) are refused by name: upload the Transactions export. Rows import with the ISIN as ticker, resolved to a symbol on screen. Dividends are not in this file; add them separately.
- Interactive Brokers: Performance & Reports, Statements, Activity, CSV, English or Spanish. Stock trades and dividends import; options, futures, forex and CFDs are skipped. Withholding tax joins its dividend as the fee only when exactly one dividend and one tax row match; otherwise it stays in the skipped list. A statement with no trades imports its open positions instead: one holding per row at IBKR's average cost, dated the statement's end date. With trades, that block is ignored.
- ClickTrade / Saxo: Informes históricos, Operaciones ejecutadas (Trades executed), as Excel (CSV also works). Spanish and English headers; title rows above the table are skipped. Known Saxo codes map to Yahoo symbols (TEF:xmce becomes TEF.MC); unknown ones import under the ISIN. Dividends are not in this report.

## Preview, then commit
Nothing is written to your portfolio until you press Confirm into the portfolio. After reading the file, the Import page shows a preview in tiers: Importable (the rows that will be written, including those with warnings), With warnings, Rejected (quarantined, never imported, for example a sale larger than the position held) and Skipped (left out on purpose, with a reason each, such as cash movements, fees, options or unreadable rows). Rows needing manual action are flagged "Action needed". Fix a rejected row in the statement and upload again. If the file names no broker (a generic CSV or an AI-read file), you must name the broker before confirming; the Fees and Custody views group your book by it. When you confirm, the rows you saw are checked again against the ledger as it is now, so a row that became a duplicate in between is still flagged. A Last import note then remembers the batch so you can undo it.

## Paste the text, encodings, delimiters and limits
If the browser or a work device blocks the file picker (or the upload fails with a 403), open "No upload? Paste the statement instead" and paste the whole export, header line included; it is parsed, previewed and committed exactly like an upload. A PDF or XLSX cannot be pasted: upload it, or export a CSV. CSV text is read as UTF-8 (with or without BOM) and falls back to the Windows codepage, so accented headers such as "Comisión" work. DEGIRO, Revolut, Revolut crypto, Trading 212 and the generic CSV detect comma, semicolon, tab or pipe separators automatically; ClickTrade CSV accepts comma or semicolon. Interactive Brokers CSV is comma-only: a semicolon-separated IBKR file will not parse. Numbers like 1.234,56 are understood in the European exports. Files over 50 MB are refused before upload: export a narrower date range.

## Re-importing and duplicates
The app flags a row as a duplicate when the ledger already holds a row with the same date, ticker, action, quantity and price, or when it repeats within the same file. A buy or sell with the same date, ticker and quantity but a different price gets a near-duplicate warning (probably the same trade read twice). Rows already in your portfolio are left out by default, on the Import page and in the chat alike: they appear in their own "Already in your portfolio" group and are not written, so re-importing an overlapping statement adds only what is new. If they really are separate movements (two identical fills on one day), tick "Import these too" on the page, or "Import these too" on the chat card. To replace the whole book instead, tick "Empty the portfolio before importing" (you must type your email; this cannot be undone); the preview is then checked against an empty ledger, so nothing is flagged. Two real partial fills of one order on the same day are kept if the position needs them.

## Importing crypto from any exchange
Revolut crypto has its own importer. For any other exchange or wallet, just drop its export in the Import page or attach it in the chat: the assistant works out which columns are the date, coin, quantity, price and so on (it names the columns; Python then does all the arithmetic, so no price is invented). Exports from Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Bitpanda, Crypto.com, KuCoin, Bitstamp, Nexo and Koinly with known headers are read without the AI; others go through the AI. Coins are stored as pairs in the file's currency (BTC-EUR, never bare BTC). A coin-to-coin swap is booked as a sale plus a purchase at that day's market value; staking, interest and airdrop rewards as a purchase at that day's price plus an income row; a deposit of coins as a transfer in at that day's value (edit it if you know the real cost); a withdrawal as a transfer out. Money deposits, internal staking moves and anything that cannot be priced that day are listed as skipped. Revolut crypto quirk: Revolut's Quantity is what the whole value would buy, but the fee is taken out first. The importer books the coins that actually arrived, so selling everything leaves nothing behind.

## Transfers between brokers
Moving shares from one broker to another is not a sale and triggers no tax, but statements often print it as one (DEGIRO books a transfer-out as a sale at the market price), which would show a gain nobody made. DEGIRO rows that are clearly a transfer (no order id, no venue, no charge) import as transfers directly. Other cases are found afterwards: on the Import page, Repairs lists "Transfers between brokers" when the same number of shares left one broker and arrived at another with the original cost. This works even if one statement names the company by ISIN (DEGIRO) and the other by symbol (IBKR). "Record as transfers" removes the false gain, keeps the original purchase dates and cost, and unifies the labels; it can be undone. Shares that arrive with no matching departure count as an opening balance at the reported cost. You can also ask the assistant to check your book: it proposes fixes for transfers, one company under two labels, the same trade imported from two brokers and Revolut crypto fee leftovers, and applies them only after you approve; they can be undone.

## Example statement, clear last import, demo and wipe
- Load an example statement: the Import page offers a real Revolut sample to an account that has never imported (only Revolut has one). It goes through the same read, preview and confirm.
- Clear last import: deletes exactly the rows of the most recent import, by id, without re-uploading and without touching anything else. Only the latest import can be undone this way. Dismiss the note instead to keep the rows.
- Use demo transactions: on the Portfolio page, an empty account can fill itself with a made-up book of seven well-known US stocks so every tab has something in it. Rows are labelled demo, and your first real import deletes them; so does Remove demo data.
- Delete all: removes every transaction (you must type your email). Positions, gains and tax reports reset to empty; it cannot be undone.

## Symbols Yahoo does not list
Some brokers print their own code for a stock (for example Revolut's SAN, ALV or a European ticker). If Yahoo does not list a code, the row still imports, but the preview warns that it will be held at cost with no price: the position shows what you paid, not a market value, and charts and returns carry it at cost. On the Import page you can pick the market: choose Pick its market, search by company name (such as Merlin Properties), and the app shows each venue's close on the days of your trades. Only a line whose price matches your fills can be selected; the choice is remembered for that code. Or choose Leave unpriced. A bare code traded in a non-dollar currency is also matched to the exchange line its price fits (Revolut's euro ALV becomes ALV.DE). If Yahoo is rate-limiting, "no price" may just mean it did not answer; try again in a minute.
