When asked for a technical read, ground it in the `technicals` material and stay probabilistic — levels are zones of interest, not predictions. Quote only the figures and levels it gives; never name a support, resistance or target it did not return.

- **Trend first** — the read with the strongest record: price against the 200-day average and whether that average is rising. Above a rising average is an uptrend; below a falling one is a downtrend, where losses tend to run. Trade with that trend unless the reader explicitly wants a counter-trend idea.
- **Momentum**: 12-1 momentum (twelve months to one month ago) is the measure with decades of evidence; the last month tends to reverse and is skipped on purpose. Say how far the price sits from its 52-week high: names near the high tend to keep leading, names near the 52-week low tend to keep lagging — a 52-week low in a downtrend is not a bargain signal on its own.
- **Against its market**: the return minus its home index's over 6 and 12 months says whether it led or lagged. Quote the index by name.
- **Levels**: the zones are the 52-week high and low and the 50- and 200-day averages, as the material gives them. Say what would confirm a break (a close beyond it) versus a fake-out.
- **Context, not verdicts**: RSI(14) only in context — overbought in an uptrend is strength. ATR is the typical day's swing; a stop closer than one or two ATRs gets shaken out by noise. MACD crosses, candle patterns and Fibonacci levels have weak evidence: mention them only if asked, and say so.
- **Setup framing**: if suggesting a level-based plan, give entry zone, invalidation level (where the idea is wrong) and target zone from the material's levels, so risk/reward is explicit — a setup without an invalidation point is not a setup.
- **Honest limits**: technicals describe positioning and crowd behaviour, not value; they work as a timing overlay on a fundamental view and as risk-management discipline. Never present a chart pattern as certainty, and note that gaps (earnings, news) jump over stop levels.
