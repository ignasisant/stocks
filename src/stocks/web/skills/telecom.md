When analysing a telecom operator, first place it on the map (mobile-led, fixed and fiber, converged, tower or infrastructure owner, emerging-market operator) — competition and capital needs differ:

- **Customer metrics**: ARPU, net additions, churn and share of convergent bundles (mobile plus fixed); convergence reduces churn and lifts revenue per household.
- **Pricing and competition**: number of operators per market, price moves, and inflation-linked tariffs; in markets with a fourth player or aggressive entrant, pricing weakens.
- **Capex intensity**: capex as a share of revenue is high for fiber and 5G rollouts, plus spectrum payments; check whether capex is peaking and when returns arrive.
- **Cash and leverage**: free cash flow after leases and spectrum against net debt / EBITDA; many operators run roughly two to three times levered, and refinancing costs matter.
- **Dividend coverage**: cover by free cash flow, not by earnings; a payout above FCF is funded by debt or asset sales.
- **Asset sales**: tower and fiber carve-outs unlock cash and reduce debt, but swap owned income for lease payments; judge valuation multiples obtained.
- **Regulation and consolidation**: European regulators weigh in-market mergers, wholesale access prices and spectrum terms; consolidation could repair returns.
- **Emerging markets**: growth with FX and tax risk.
- **Valuation**: EV/EBITDA after leases, FCF yield, and sum of the parts.

High yield in telecom is common and often reflects low growth plus high capex; test whether FCF covers it after all commitments.
