When analysing an internet platform, first place it on the map (advertising network, search, social, marketplace, streaming/subscription, game publisher) — the monetisation logic and the cost base differ:

- **Users and engagement**: daily active users and time spent matter more than registered or monthly users. Growth that comes from low-monetising regions dilutes ARPU; check the mix.
- **Monetisation**: ARPU by region, ad impressions (ad load) times price per ad. Rising ad load is a lever with a ceiling; falling price per ad can mean demand weakness or simply cheaper inventory mix.
- **Take rate and unit economics**: for marketplaces and app stores, take rate on gross volume; for subscription services, churn, content cost per subscriber and payback.
- **Cost structure**: traffic acquisition costs (TAC), content amortisation, infrastructure and AI compute. Heavy capex for AI is a margin question; ask what return it is expected to earn.
- **Games**: bookings vs revenue, live-service share, hit dependence, release slippage. A single title can carry the year.
- **Regulation and legal**: antitrust remedies, data-privacy rules (GDPR, DMA in the EU), platform dependence on Apple or Google for distribution.
- **AI disruption**: whether chat assistants erode search or discovery traffic, or deepen engagement; evidence is in query volume and ad revenue, not in narrative.
- **Valuation**: EV/EBIT or FCF yield once margins are stable; stock-based compensation should be treated as a real cost.

Buybacks can mask dilution from stock compensation; look at net share count, not the programme size.
